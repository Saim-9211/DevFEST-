1. **Problem Statement**

Households in Pakistan often purchase groceries individually, even though many families live in the same apartment buildings and buy similar essential products every month. Individual purchases limit their bargaining power, forcing residents to pay retail prices instead of benefiting from wholesale rates. Meanwhile, suppliers and wholesalers lack an efficient way to aggregate demand from these households.

2. **Proposed Solution**

We aim to build a community-based grocery procurement platform that enables residents of the same apartment building to combine their monthly grocery orders. By aggregating demand, we can negotiate bulk prices directly with wholesalers and suppliers, passing the savings on to residents through discounted grocery packages.

3. **Target Audience**

- Residents of apartment buildings and residential communities.
- Grocery wholesalers and distributors.
- Suppliers and manufacturers of household essentials.

4. **How It Works**

1. Residents join their apartment building's group on the platform.
2. Each household selects its monthly grocery requirements.
3. The platform aggregates orders from all participating residents.
4. The combined demand is used to negotiate wholesale prices.
5. Groceries are delivered collectively to the apartment building and distributed among residents.

5. **Expected Impact**

- Lower monthly grocery expenses for households.
- Greater purchasing power through collective demand.
- Direct connections between suppliers and consumers.
- Reduced dependence on traditional retail intermediaries.

**Core Value Proposition:** Making wholesale grocery prices accessible to ordinary households through collective purchasing. Even orders worth as little as PKR 2,000 could potentially qualify for discounts of 20–30%.

**Important:** Treat the proposed 20–30% savings as a target to validate through supplier negotiations rather than a guaranteed discount.
